Personal Nutrition Guidelines (Sample Document)

This is a small self-contained reference document used by the RAG evaluation
scenarios. The facts below are the ground truth that "search the document"
scenarios are scored against. Swap this content for your own if you wish, but
update the matching RAG scenarios in scenarios.yaml accordingly.

1. Daily protein target
   Aim for 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight on training days.

2. Hydration
   Drink at least 2.5 litres of water per day. Add 500 ml for every hour of
   intense exercise.

3. Pre-workout meal
   Eat a meal containing complex carbohydrates roughly 2 hours before training.
   A good example is oatmeal with banana.

4. Post-workout window
   Consume protein and carbohydrates within 45 minutes after finishing a
   workout to support recovery.

5. Recommended weekly training volume
   Perform at least 150 minutes of moderate cardio per week, split across at
   least three sessions.

Note: This document does NOT contain any information about supplements,
medication dosages, or specific brand recommendations. Questions about those
topics cannot be answered from this document.
