FitZone Gym Membership Terms (Sample Document)

This is a small self-contained reference document used by the RAG evaluation
scenarios. The facts below are the ground truth that "search the document"
scenarios are scored against.

Membership tiers:
  - Basic:    35 BGN per month. Access to the weights area only.
  - Standard: 55 BGN per month. Adds group classes and the swimming pool.
  - Premium:  85 BGN per month. Adds a monthly session with a personal trainer.

Opening hours:
  - Weekdays:  06:00 to 23:00
  - Weekends:  08:00 to 20:00

Cancellation policy:
  Memberships can be cancelled with 30 days written notice. No cancellation fee
  applies after the first 3 months.

Note: This document does NOT mention any student discount or any free trial
period. Questions about those topics cannot be answered from this document.
